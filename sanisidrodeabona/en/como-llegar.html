Getting here and around

San Isidro is in the south of Tenerife, very close to the airport and the motorway.

From Tenerife South airport

It is the nearest airport and the drive is short.

By car

The TF-1 motorway links the area with the rest of the island. It is the most convenient way to get around.

By bus

There are TITSA bus connections (called "guagua" in the Canaries) to other areas. Check timetables on their website, as they change.
