Things to do in San Isidro de Abona

A quiet village in the south of Tenerife, well placed for exploring the island.

Stroll around the village

San Isidro keeps the feel of a southern village: quiet streets, local life and good Canarian food.

Festivals

The village celebrates its festivities in honour of San Isidro Labrador (Saint Isidore the Farmer).

Trips from here

From San Isidro you can easily reach the southern beaches, Mount Teide and other parts of the island thanks to the TF-1 motorway.
