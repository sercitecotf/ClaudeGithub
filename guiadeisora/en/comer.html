Where to eat in Guía de Isora

Canarian cuisine, catch of the day and quiet places with sea views.

What to try

  • Papas arrugadas with mojo (wrinkled potatoes with red and green sauce).
  • Fresh fish of the day, especially on the coast.
  • Grilled cheese with mojo.
  • Goat meat and traditional dishes from the mid-altitude villages.

Playa San Juan

An area with several restaurants by the harbour, specialising in fish and seafood.

Inland villages

In the inland villages you will find traditional cooking at more moderate prices.
