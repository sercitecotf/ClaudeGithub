Beaches and coves in Guía de Isora

The municipality's coastline is volcanic rock, with small beaches, natural pools and fishing harbours.

Playa San Juan

A fishing village with its harbour, seafront promenade and a good choice of restaurants. A great starting point if you want local atmosphere and services close to the sea.

Alcalá

A small, quiet coastal village known for its bathing spots among the rocks. Ideal if you prefer a calmer atmosphere.

Playa de Abama

A beach in the Abama area on the municipality's coast. Check the state of access and the sea conditions before you go.

Tip: the Atlantic on this coast can have swell and currents. Always check the flags and the sea forecast before swimming.
