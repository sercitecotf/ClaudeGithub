Tours and activities

Guía de Isora is well placed for exploring the west and south of Tenerife.

Whale and dolphin watching

Tenerife's southwest coast is one of the best-known areas in Spain for spotting dolphins and pilot whales. Boat trips leave from nearby harbours; choose operators with a responsible-tourism certification.

See whale-watching tours

Los Gigantes and the cliffs

A short drive away you will find the Los Gigantes cliffs, with viewpoints and boat trips.

Mount Teide

From here you can organise a day trip to Teide National Park. Remember that a permit is required to climb to the summit beyond the refuge.

Hiking in the mid-altitude villages

The paths through the municipality's mid-altitude villages offer very different scenery from the coast. Specific routes with distance and difficulty are still to be added.
