Getting here and around

Guía de Isora is on Tenerife's southwest coast, well connected by road.

From the airport

The nearest airport is Tenerife South (TFS), about 30-40 minutes by car. From Tenerife North the journey is considerably longer.

By car

This is the most convenient way to move between beaches, viewpoints and villages. Book ahead in high season.

By bus

There are bus connections (called "guagua" in the Canaries, operated by TITSA) to other parts of the island. Check timetables and routes on their website before travelling, as they change.

Tip: roads in the area are winding and hilly; always allow more time than your GPS says.
