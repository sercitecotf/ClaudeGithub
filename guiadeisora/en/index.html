About Guía de Isora

Guía de Isora is a municipality on the southwest coast of Tenerife. It combines mid-altitude villages with quiet coastal spots such as Playa San Juan and Alcalá, enjoys great weather almost all year round and is a convenient base for Los Gigantes, Mount Teide and the south of the island.

We write this guide from Tenerife, so you can plan your visit with clear and useful information.
